★ Tarapirohe Conservation Knowledge (All 3 Facts) ★

1. Braided River Flyers

Tarapirohe (Black-fronted terns) nest on braided riverbeds in Aotearoa NZ and teach their chicks to fly and catch insects over water.

2. Ground Predators

Introduced predators like stoats, rats, and hedgehogs threaten Tarapirohe nests and young chicks resting on river stones.

3. Visitors & Pets

Unleashed dogs and roaming cats can disturb or harm Tarapirohe families. Keeping pets controlled helps chicks survive!
